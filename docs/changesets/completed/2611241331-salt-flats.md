# Salt Flats for the Brine Well

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611241331-salt-flats

## Summary

Deserts gain salt flats: patches of a new walkable, buildable terrain. The Brine Well must stand
wholly on salt flat instead of on any desert sand.

## Motivation

The Brine Well's tooltip says it draws from desert salt flats, but its rule (OnDesertSand) accepts
any Sand in a Desert map cell, which is most of every desert. Real salt flats make it a placement
the player has to find, like a motherlode, and make the tooltip true.

## Design

- **Terrain:** `TerrainType.SaltFlat`: walkable at speed 1 (hard, flat crust), buildable, takes
  roads at 1 Stone a cell, and takes no features, rolled fields, or motherlodes. Its look is a
  warm off-white crust (apart from snow's cool white) with low ridges along an irregular hexagon
  pattern, nearly level. It ranks just under Sand in draw priority (Grass 7, Snow 6, Sand 5,
  SaltFlat 4, the rest as before), and a generated flat borders only Sand, so Sand draws its edges
  over the flat and the flat needs ground tiles only, no transition pieces.
- **Generation:** placed sites rather than a noise field (a threshold on noise gave a long tail of
  flats 15 or more cells across). Each square of 18 map cells holds one site, an ellipse with
  hashed center and half-widths of 1.75 to 3 map cells, its edge roughened by noise. A cell inside
  becomes SaltFlat when it and its 8 neighbors are Desert Sand and it lies in a 2 x 2 block of such
  cells. Measured over six seeds: one flat per about 380 desert map cells, 2 to 6 cells across
  (median 5), about 14 cells each (Crude Oil fields run about one per 190 desert cells). The rate
  is a first guess, to tune by play.
- **Rivers:** a flat takes no river, so a river line reaching one stops at its rim (ends or pools),
  like a desert river drying into a basin.
- **Placement:** the Brine Well's rule becomes OnSaltFlat: every footprint cell is SaltFlat. The
  biome no longer matters.
- **Old saves:** chunks are saved in full, so explored and generated ground keeps its terrain and
  a Brine Well already built stands where it is; chunks generated after this change have flats,
  which may stop short at a border with an older chunk.
- **Fixed map:** the Desert region gains a 2 x 2 flat at x 14 to 15, z 5 to 6.

## Decisions

1. **Terrain or site:** terrain; several wells may share a flat. A flat never runs out (lithium
   brine does not deplete on a game's timescale); the well's rate against demand is the balance.
2. **Size and frequency:** 3 x 3 to 6 x 6 map cells, one per 400 desert map cells.
3. **Other uses:** only the Brine Well's ground for now.

## Open questions (answered)

1. **Terrain or site:** a new terrain patch (proposed), or a motherlode-style site, one Brine Well
   per flat?
2. **Size and frequency:** a few 3 x 3 to 6 x 6 flats per desert at one per 300 desert map cells
   (proposed), or fewer and larger?
3. **Other uses:** anything besides the Brine Well's ground?

## Code changes

- `src/Telaelus.Sim/TerrainType.cs`, `TerrainDefs.cs`: `SaltFlat` and its def; the priorities
  renumbered to fit it under Sand.
- `src/Telaelus.Sim/CapacityType.cs`, `BuildingDefs.cs`, `Simulation.cs`: `Placement.OnDesertSand`
  becomes `OnSaltFlat`.
- `src/Telaelus.Sim/WorldGenerator.cs`: `WithSaltFlats` and `InSaltFlatSite` (`SaltFlatSpacing`,
  `SaltFlatMinRadius`, `SaltFlatMaxRadius`), run after the base terrain; deposit and motherlode
  candidates skip salt flats.
- `src/Telaelus.Sim/SimulationTerrain.cs`: the fixed map's flat.
- `src/Telaelus/Presentation/TerrainTiles.cs`: the `salt_flat` file name.
- `src/asset-factory/tools/terrain_gen.py` (`salt_cracks`, `build_salt_flat`, run after the
  others so no existing model's seed moves), `mesh.py` (`salt_flat`, `salt_crack` colors),
  `arrange.py` (the priority and a flat in the ground arrangement); the models
  `ground_salt_flat_a|b|c` (`.obj`, `.mtl`, shipped `.glb` and `.import`); kept previews
  `previews/ground_salt_flat.png` and a rerendered `previews/arrangement_ground.png`.
- Tests: `SaltFlatTests` (frequency and size over four seeds; desert only, bordered by Sand, 2 x 2
  blocks; no field, motherlode, or feature on a flat; the fixed map's flat); `LaterTiersTests` (the
  Brine Well refused on Desert Sand and off the flat, accepted on it); the `later-tiers` scenario
  paints a flat under its Brine Well; `terrain-types` paints a flat in a ring of sand, checks the
  terrain under the player, and screenshots it; the scenario schema gains `SaltFlat`.

## Implementation notes

- Not checked on screen: a flat in a generated desert, and the Brine Well's ghost going green on a
  flat and red off it (the placement rule is covered by `LaterTiersTests`).
- A flat painted by `Simulation.Debug` beside Rock, Mud, Lava, or water would need
  `edge_salt_flat` pieces that do not exist and fail to draw; generation never places one there.
  Painting next to Grass, Snow, or Sand is fine.
