# README Showcase

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611262141-readme-showcase

## Summary

A `showcase` scenario builds a busy, fully working town for a screenshot, and that screenshot goes
at the top of the README's "The game" section, so a visitor sees what the game looks like before
reading about it.

## Motivation

The README describes the game in words only. The demo save is the closest thing to a showcase,
but it is an early mining town (Mines, Smelters, hubs, a road) and shows none of the later tiers.
A scripted scenario makes the picture reproducible: when the models or the look change, rerunning
it gives a fresh screenshot of the same town.

## Design

- **The world:** the demo save's generated world (seed 20261116), around the HQ, with the sea to
  the north and a pond to the east. The world's own fields on the town's ground are cleared, and
  the town's fields are placed where its extractors need them. A sand beach on the coast and a
  salt flat are painted for the Sand Collectors and the Brine Well. The fog is lifted by standing
  the player at 16 points across the ground.
- **The town:** 55 buildings of 36 types, placed free with `placeBuilding`, several at level 2 or
  3 so their upgraded models show (Drill Rig, Deep Mine, Steel Mill, Electric Smelter, Cement
  Kiln, Lumber Mill, Oil Rig, Pumping Stations, Sand Sifter and Dredge):
  - North of the HQ: Research Lab, Glassworks, Electrolyzer, Fuel Plant, Chemical Plant, a Tank
    Farm, two Silos, and on the coast the Water Pumps and Sand Collectors.
  - West: three Mines, three Smelters, a Depot, Warehouses, a Stockyard, and a Vault.
  - East: a Stone Mine and Rock Crusher, a Limestone Mine, Cement Kiln, and Concrete Mixer, the
    Brine Well, and three Burner Generators.
  - Further east: an Oil Rig, Refinery, and two Tank Farms; a Fabricator, Electronics Works, and
    Semiconductor Fab.
  - South of the road: the Shipyard, Assembly Plant, Fission Reactor, Enrichment Plant, four Solar
    Arrays, two Battery Banks, and a forest with a Lumber Mill, Sawmill, and Forester.
  - Two Small Hubs bring the far east online; a road runs past the HQ.
- **All working:** the providers are placed first and given a second, since the Fission Reactor
  needs the Water that the powered Pumping Stations supply. Every queueing processor gets an
  order of 99, the stockpile is seeded with ten cycles of each order's inputs plus fuel, Material
  Transfer is completed so the extractors deliver straight to the stockpile, and Steel Works runs
  in the Research Lab. After 20 seconds at 10:55 every building is online and working, and power
  (607 of 745), water (33 of 42), and sand are normal.
- **The shot:** camera zoom 50 centered beside the HQ, 1280 x 720 with the HUD, as every scenario
  screenshot is. Its checks (power, water, and sand normal; Steel Works running; the last building
  online) also run in the integration tests, so a change that breaks the town shows up there.
- **The README image:** the screenshot is committed as `docs/images/showcase.png` and shown at the
  start of the README's "The game" section, before its description. It is regenerated by hand
  (rerun the scenario windowed and copy the PNG), not by CI.
