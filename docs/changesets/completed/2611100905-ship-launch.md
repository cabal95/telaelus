# Ship launch

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611100905-ship-launch
- Depends-on: none

## Summary

Add the ship launch win condition that closes the initial on-planet loop.

## Motivation

Give the production chain a goal: accumulate enough refined output to launch a ship.

## Design

- When the stockpile holds at least 500 Fuel and 100 Metal, launching is available. Pressing `L`
  consumes that cost and records a launched ship (`ShipsLaunched`).
- The HUD shows launch progress (Fuel and Metal toward the cost), a prompt when ready, and the
  launched count.

## Code changes

- `Simulation`: `LaunchFuelCost` (500), `LaunchMetalCost` (100), `ShipsLaunched`, `CanLaunchShip`,
  `TryLaunchShip`.
- `GameRoot`: handle the `L` key as the launch command. `Hud`: the launch line.

## Blueprint updates

- Updates `buildings.md` (the ship launch win condition).

## Verification

Accumulate the resources; the HUD shows the prompt; pressing `L` consumes them and increments the
launched count.
