# Production Tiers 3: The Later Tiers

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611151049-later-tiers
- Depends-on: 2611151033-first-ship-parts

## Summary

The third production-tier changeset: every catalog item that nothing makes yet, and the buildings
that make them. Tier 1 gains Lumber, Cement, Concrete, Sulfuric Acid, Enriched Uranium, Lithium
(from a **Brine Well**), and a second Liquid Oxygen source (the **Air Separator**). Tier 2 gains
Insulated Cable, Silicon Wafer, Processor, Magnet, Electric Motor, Battery, Solar Cell, and Armor
Plate. Tier 3 gains Computer, Heat Shield, Fuel Rod, Life Support Unit, and Sensor Array. Most go
into existing buildings as new recipes, as the first ship parts changeset planned; seven new
buildings cover the rest.

## Motivation

The last of the three changesets 2611151006-production-refining set out (its item 3, "the later
tiers"). After the first ship, 23 catalog items have no recipe, Lithium has no source, and Liquid
Oxygen still comes only from the Electrolyzer's Water. Part B's ship parts (Maybe in
2611141757-resource-tree) will consume most of these; until then, the new buildings' costs do
(see "What consumes them" below).

## Design

### Scope

- **In:** the recipes below; new recipes in the Chemical Plant, Fabricator, Electronics Works, and
  Assembly Plant; seven new buildings and their models; the Brine Well's placement; Lumber and
  Concrete as building materials in the new buildings' costs.
- **Out:** Part B ship parts and consumables (Solar Array, Battery Bank, Fission Reactor, Sensor
  Suite, and so on); generators that burn Fuel Rods or run on Solar Cells (see the open
  questions); a second ship or a changed ship bill; rebalancing existing buildings' costs.

### Tier 1: materials (placeholders)

| Item | Inputs | Makes | Building | Cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lumber | 2 Wood | 1 | **Sawmill** (new) | 8 s |
| Cement | 2 Limestone, burns fuel | 1 | **Cement Kiln** (new) | 12 s |
| Concrete | 1 Cement (Sand and Water capacity) | 2 | **Concrete Mixer** (new) | 10 s |
| Sulfuric Acid | 1 Sulfur (the plant's Water) | 2 | Chemical Plant | 8 s |
| Enriched Uranium | 2 Uranium Ore, 1 Sulfuric Acid | 1 | **Enrichment Plant** (new) | 30 s |
| Lithium | none | 1 | **Brine Well** (new) | 10 s |
| Liquid Oxygen | none | 1 | **Air Separator** (new) | 5 s |

- **The Brine Well** is a collector like the Quarry: endless Lithium, no field. It stands on Sand in
  a Desert map cell (`Placement.OnDesertSand`: every cell Sand terrain, and the map cell's biome
  Desert), the plan's "salt flats" without a new terrain type.
- **The Air Separator** makes Liquid Oxygen from air with Power and no Water, so a base can move
  its oxygen off the Electrolyzer and its Water.

  there is more than one), an output buffer of 10, 5 cycles of each input.
- The Cement Kiln burns fuel like the Glassworks (Coal, Charcoal, or Wood, Best by default) and
  needs no Power, so Cement and Concrete come early.

### What consumes them

Until Part B, several new items would be dead ends. The new buildings' costs give each a use, and
the order of the chain falls out of them:

- **Lumber** builds the Cement Kiln and Concrete Mixer; **Concrete** builds every tier-1-and-up
  new building.
- **Electric Motors** build the Air Separator and the Enrichment Plant; **Armor Plate** and
  **Computers** the Enrichment Plant.
- Batteries, Solar Cells, Heat Shields, Life Support Units, Sensor Arrays, and Fuel Rods have no
  consumer yet (see the open questions).

### UI

Nothing new: the Recipe picker, display stand, panels, and capacity rows from the earlier
changesets cover every new building. The Brine Well's placement tooltip names its rule ("Needs
Sand in a Desert").

### Assets

Seven building models with accent colors, kept preview sheets, and README rows (every item already
has its item model and icon):

- **Sawmill (3 x 5):** an open-sided plank shed over a long saw bench, logs feeding in at the back,
  stacked planks at the front (`lumber_accent`, a fresh-cut tan).
- **Cement Kiln (3 x 7):** a long sloped rotary kiln drum on piers, a preheater tower and chimney
  at the back, a clinker pile at the front (`cement_accent`, a pale grey-beige).
- **Concrete Mixer (3 x 4):** a raised cement silo, a mixing drum on a frame, a chute at the front
  (`cement_accent` with `power_accent`).
- **Brine Well (4 x 6):** a small wellhead and pump at the back and two shallow evaporation ponds
  in pale blue-white down the front, a salt heap (`lithium_accent`, a pale lilac).
- **Semiconductor Fab (5 x 6):** a sealed white clean-room block with a stepped roof of air
  handlers, a chemical tank, a loading door at the front (`fab_accent`, a clean violet-blue).
- **Air Separator (4 x 5):** two tall cold boxes, a compressor house, and a frosted Liquid Oxygen
  tank at the front (`gas_accent`, shared with the Electrolyzer).
- **Enrichment Plant (6 x 7):** a long hall of centrifuge cascades under a raised roof, a
  containment dome at the back, a cask pad at the front (`nuclear_accent`, a hazard yellow-green).

## Verification

- Unit tests: each building's recipe list; a two-unit recipe (Silicon Wafer); the Brine Well's
  placement (on Desert Sand, refused on Sand elsewhere and on Desert grass) and its Lithium; the Air
  Separator's Liquid Oxygen with no Water; the Cement Kiln burning fuel; the Concrete Mixer drawing
  Sand and Water; an end-to-end run from seeded tier-1 materials to a Computer and a Fuel Rod.
- Scenario: `later-tiers` places a Sawmill, Cement Kiln, and Concrete Mixer and sees Concrete made,
  places a Brine Well on Desert Sand and sees Lithium, and checks a Semiconductor Fab's display
  stand change with its recipe.
- Both test suites pass; a windowed run shows the seven models.

## Decisions

1. **Recipe homes:** a new Semiconductor Fab makes Silicon Wafers, Processors, and Solar Cells; the
   Fabricator takes Magnet, Electric Motor, and Armor Plate (eight recipes); the Electronics Works
   takes Insulated Cable and Battery. Electric Motors stay tier 2 so the Air Separator does not wait
   for the Assembly Plant.
2. **Items with no use yet:** Batteries, Solar Cells, Heat Shields, Life Support Units, Sensor
   Arrays, and Fuel Rods are made here with no consumer. A fourth changeset adds Part A uses (a
   Solar Array generator, a Battery Bank that stores Power, a Fission Reactor that burns Fuel Rods).
3. **Building materials:** Lumber and Concrete go only into the new buildings' costs; moving
   existing buildings to them waits for a balancing pass.
4. **Brine Well placement:** every cell on Sand terrain in a Desert map cell
   (`Placement.OnDesertSand`); no new terrain type.
5. **Two Liquid Oxygen sources:** the Electrolyzer keeps its Liquid Oxygen recipe beside the Air
   Separator.

## Implementation notes

- **The Brine Well and Air Separator are processors** with a no-input recipe (as the Electrolyzer
  makes its gases), not fieldless collectors like the Quarry: the same behavior with no new
  collector code. Neither has a display stand (one recipe each).
- **Lithium keeps its kind** (`TerrainSourced`, with no room at the HQ): a Brine Well's Lithium
  needs a Stockyard or Silo, as Quartz does. `ResourceDefs` is unchanged.
- **No placement-reason text:** the game shows no reason for any placement rule (the ghost turns
  red), so the Brine Well gets none either.
- **The Sawmill reuses `lumber_accent`** (the Lumber Camp's brown) rather than a new tan; a `brine`
  palette entry colors its ponds.
- **The scenario** covers the Sawmill, Cement Kiln, Concrete Mixer, Brine Well, and Semiconductor
  Fab; the Air Separator and Enrichment Plant are covered by the unit tests.
