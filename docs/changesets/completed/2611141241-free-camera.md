# Free Camera

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611141241-free-camera
- Depends-on: none (lands after 2611141051-fog-of-war, whose explored area bounds the pan)

## Summary

The player can drag the camera around the map without moving the character, to look at and
click on buildings and fields elsewhere, or to find the way back to something. As soon as the
character moves, the camera returns: it slides back over a few frames if the character is still
on screen, and snaps back if not.

## Motivation

The camera is locked to the player, so anything off screen can only be reached by walking there.
With more buildings spread out, checking on one means a trip. Panning also helps plan a route
back to a deposit or a building the player remembers.

## Design

### Panning

- **Drag the map** with the right mouse button held, or with the left button while Ctrl is held
  (laptop touchpads have no easy right-button drag): the ground point under the pointer stays
  under the pointer as it moves (grab and drag), at any zoom. Either one is enough. A Ctrl + left
  drag never selects, clicks, or places, even in build mode.
- There is no edge scrolling (the camera moving when the pointer nears the window's edge): it is
  easy to trigger by accident while reaching for the top bar or a panel.
- A drag starts only over the world: over a panel, modal, or the top bar it does nothing (the
  dialogs already take those clicks).
- A drag of a few pixels or less counts as no drag, so a right click stays free for later uses
  (and a Ctrl + left click does nothing).
- **Bounds:** the camera's center stays inside the explored area's bounding box (the smallest
  rectangle of map cells holding every explored cell), so panning never shows ground the player
  has not seen beyond the fog's edge. In the bounded test map without fog, the map's own
  rectangle.
- Zooming while panned zooms about the screen center, as now.
- Panning changes nothing in the sim: it is presentation only (and per player in co-op).

### Coming back

- The camera is **detached** from the moment a drag moves it until it returns.
- When the player gives move input (WASD or the arrows) while it is detached, even if the
  character is blocked and does not actually move:
  - if the character is **on screen** (inside the visible view, ignoring the area covered by an
    open panel), the camera slides back to center on them, easing out over 0.25 s (about 8 frames
    at 30 fps), and follows normally from there;
  - if the character is **off screen**, the camera jumps straight back to them.
- Harvesting, building, and opening dialogs do not bring it back; only moving does.
- **Space** also brings the camera back to the character (sliding or snapping by the same rule),
  without moving them.
- While attached, the camera follows the character exactly as it does today.

### Interaction while panned

- Hover, click, the building and field panels, and building placement all work wherever the
  camera looks, since they already ray-cast from the pointer through the camera. Placing a
  building far from the character is allowed: the rules have no distance limit today, and a build
  range would be a rule change for its own changeset.
- Harvesting still needs the character beside a field.

### Testing

- Snapshot: `camera.target` [x, z] (the ground point at the screen center) and `camera.detached`.
- A scenario command `drag { "button": "right" | "left", "ctrl": bool, "from": [x, y], "to": [x, y], "frames": n }`
  (screen points in pixels of the 1280 x 720 test window), pushed as real mouse input like
  `click`.
- A `free-camera` scenario: a right drag moves the camera and not the player, and the ground
  point stays under the pointer; a small drag does nothing; a drag over the build menu does
  nothing; the camera stops at the explored area's edge; moving with the character on screen
  slides the camera back over about 8 frames (`camera.target` part way after 3 frames, centered
  after 10); moving with the character off screen snaps back on the next frame; Space returns it
  without moving the character; a Ctrl + left drag pans the same way and, in build mode, places
  nothing; a building can be clicked and its panel opened while panned.

## Code changes

- `CameraRig`: it now looks at a ground point (`GroundCenter`) and places itself from it, with the
  attached and detached states, the drag (`BeginDrag`, `DragTo`, `CancelDrag`: the grabbed
  ground point stays under the pointer, after a 4-pixel threshold), the slide back (ease-out over
  0.25 s toward the player's current position) and the jump, the pan bounds (a `Bounds`
  callback), and `Detached`. `GameRoot` still sets the player's position as its target each
  frame.
- `GameRoot`: a right press or a Ctrl + left press that reaches the world starts a drag; while it
  is held, `_Input` (before the UI) follows its motion and release, so it keeps going over a
  panel; Space and move input call `ReturnToPlayer` with the on-screen test (the player's screen
  position inside the view and outside the open panel, from `DialogHost.PanelScreenRect`); the
  bounds come from `Simulation.ExploredBounds`.
- UI buttons (`Dialog`'s Close, the building detail's toggle and Destroy) take no keyboard focus.
  Found while testing: Godot buttons take focus when clicked, and Space presses a focused button,
  so Space after switching a building off switched it back on. The UI is mouse-driven, so no
  button needs focus.
- `FogOverlay`: no darkness at all in a world without fog. Found while testing the pan at the
  test map's edge: cells beyond a bounded map count as unexplored, so since fog-of-war the test
  map's border tiles drew a dark rim and the plane under it. (presentation.md now says the overlay
  is for worlds with fog.)
- `BuildController`: ignores left clicks with Ctrl held (they belong to the drag).
- `Simulation` (from fog-of-war): `ExploredBounds`, the explored bounding box, kept up to date as
  cells are revealed (display only), with a unit test.
- Testing: the `drag` command (parser, executor, schema), the snapshot fields, and the
  `free-camera` scenario.
