# Trainer

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611191051-trainer
- Depends-on: 2611191008-notifications

## Summary

An optional trainer walks a new player through every control and the opening loop of play in a
real new game: walking, the camera, gathering by hand, the inventory, building one Lumber Camp
(the build menu, rotating, placing), its panel, collecting its output, the network, research,
and the game menu. Each step is one instruction on a trainer card
that stays until the player does it, then checks itself off. It takes about five minutes and can
be skipped at any time.

## Motivation

Nothing in the game says how to play. The controls are not guessable (right click walks, right
drag pans, E harvests, B builds, R rotates, N shows the network), and the opening (gather Wood,
build a camp, get its output home) is the part a new player is most likely to stall on. The
notifications give us the card to show it on.

## Design

### Starting it

- **New Game asks once.** The first New Game on a machine shows a modal: "Learn to play?" with
  **Start trainer** and **Skip**. The answer is remembered (`user://settings.cfg`), so later New
  Games do not ask; the game menu gains a **Trainer** button that starts it (from the first step)
  in any game.
- The trainer runs in the normal generated world, so what it teaches is real play. The starter
  set (two Wood fields and one Stone field near the spawn) is what the gathering steps point at.

### The trainer card

- A **step card**: the lasting toast's look (accent strip, X at the top right) at the top of the
  notification stack, with three lines: "Trainer 3 / 13" in `SmallSize` `Accent`, the
  instruction in `BodySize` `Text`, and an optional hint in `SmallSize` `TextDim`. It has no
  countdown bar and never times out; it stays until its step is done.
- When a step is done the card shows a check and "Done" for 1 second, then the next step's card
  slides in. Other notifications stack below it as usual, and it is never pushed out.
- Its X asks "Stop the trainer?" (Stop / Keep going). Stopping ends it; the game menu's Trainer
  starts it again from the first step.
- **Pointers:** a step about a place puts the walk marker's ring (in `Accent`, pulsing) on it:
  the nearest starter Wood field, the Lumber Camp, the Stone field. A step about a key shows the
  key in brackets in its instruction ("Press [B]").

### The steps

Each step completes on what the player does, checked every frame; a step already done when it
comes up (the player ran ahead) completes at once.

| # | Instruction | Hint | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Right-click the ground to walk there. | Ctrl + left click works too. | The player has walked 4 units |
| 2 | Drag with the right mouse button to look around. | Ctrl + left drag works too. | The camera has panned 8 units |
| 3 | Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in and out. | | The zoom has changed both ways |
| 4 | Press [Space] to bring the view back to you. | | The view returns |
| 5 | Walk to the trees and hold [E] to gather Wood. | Gather 15 to unlock the Lumber Camp. | The Lumber Camp is unlocked |
| 6 | Press [I] to see what you have. | | The inventory opens |
| 7 | Press [B] and choose the Lumber Camp. | | Build mode with the Lumber Camp |
| 8 | Press [R] to turn it. | | It has turned once |
| 9 | Left-click near the trees to place it. | It needs trees in range and the HQ's network. [Esc] cancels. | A Lumber Camp stands |
| 10 | Click the Lumber Camp to see what it is doing. | [Esc] first leaves build mode. | Its panel opens |
| 11 | Its Wood waits in the camp. Stand by it and hold [E] to collect it. | | Wood collected from it by hand |
| 12 | Press [N] to show the network. | Buildings work only inside it. | The network overlay shows |
| 13 | Press [T] to see research, then [Esc] for the game menu. | Save, load, and quit from there. | The game menu has opened after the research tree |

- After step 13: a lasting toast, "Trainer complete", "You know the controls. Gather Stone for
  a Mine, and a Depot's trucks will haul for you."
- The steps live in one table (`TrainerSteps`: id, text, hint, pointer, check), so adding,
  removing, or reordering a step is one edit.

### State and saving

- The trainer is presentation (the sim knows nothing of it): it reads sim state, the sim's
  `Happened` events, and presentation state (camera, open dialog, build mode, the network
  overlay).
- Its place is saved with the presentation's part of a save (beside the pins and zoom): the step
  it is on, or none. Continuing a save resumes the step. The demo save has no trainer.
- The counters a step needs (distance walked, panned, zoom directions) start when the step does
  and are not saved; a resumed step starts its count again.

## Decisions

1. **Asking:** on the first New Game only; the game menu's Trainer button starts it later.
2. **Depth:** stop after the network. The Depot and road steps are dropped for now (a later
   changeset can add them as a second part); research and the game menu stay, since they are
   controls.
3. **Pointer ring:** shown on places.
4. **Steps already done:** complete at once.
5. **The card's X:** asks to stop the trainer.

## Implementation notes

- **No prompt flag.** Test runs (`--scenario=`, `--control-port=`) never ask and never touch the
  settings file, so the existing scenarios needed nothing; `askTrainer` opens the question when a
  scenario wants it.
- **The pointer ring** is its own node (`PointerRing`) rather than a mode of the walk marker, so
  both can show at once (walking to the ringed trees).
- **Step 10's hint** was added after the camp screenshot: build mode stays on after placing, so a
  left click on the camp would place another one; "[Esc] first leaves build mode."
- **The ring follows the nearest explored Wood field**, so after the player walks it may move to
  another starter field; the scenario follows it.
- **The zoom step** is driven by `cameraZoom` in the scenario (there is no world mouse-wheel
  command); it changes the zoom as the wheel does.
- **The step card** is a third toast style (`ToastStyle.Step`): the lasting card without its
  countdown bar or icon, with a header line; `Notifications.ShowStep`, `MarkStepDone`, `HideStep`.

## Open questions (answered)

1. **Asking:** prompt on the first New Game only (proposed), every New Game, or a separate
   **Trainer** button on the main menu instead of a prompt?
2. **Gameplay depth:** the proposal teaches one camp, collecting by hand, then a Depot and a road
   (steps 13 and 14). Stop after the camp and the network (12 steps, about 4 minutes), or keep the
   Depot and road (proposed), since trucks are how the opening actually scales?
3. **Pointer ring:** show it on places (proposed), or keep the trainer to text?
4. **Steps the player already did** complete at once (proposed). Or show each anyway, so nothing
   is skipped silently?
5. **The step card's X:** asks to stop the trainer (proposed), or just hides the card until the
   next step?
