# Hold Mode for Queueing Processors

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611242051-hold-mode

## Summary

A processor with a crafting queue can be switched to **Hold**: it runs one recipe and keeps its
output's stock at a number the player sets, making more whenever storage holds less and pausing
(online and still drawing its capacity) once it holds that many. Switching to Hold empties the
queue; switching back to **Queue** returns to orders.

## Motivation

Keeping a stock of Iron or Steel topped up today means watching storage and queueing more orders
by hand. Hold mode lets a Smelter keep 50 Iron on hand by itself, which is what most intermediate
production wants.

## Design

- **Which buildings:** every processor with a crafting queue (`Building.Queues`). The three that
  run without a queue (the Electrolyzer, Brine Well, and Air Separator) and extractors are
  unchanged.
- **State:** `Building.Hold` is null in Queue mode, or the held recipe index and the target (in
  items of that recipe's output).
- **Entering Hold** (`SetHold(id, recipe, target)`): the queue empties. A running cycle of the
  held recipe keeps going; one of another recipe stops and returns what it took to storage (as
  `CancelCraft` does). Calling it again in Hold changes the recipe or the target; a running cycle
  of the old recipe finishes first, as a recipe change does between orders.
- **Running in Hold:** the "quantity available" is what storage holds of the output (the stockpile
  the top bar and inventory show; buffers do not count). While it is under the target, the
  building starts a cycle whenever its inputs are at hand, as for a queue order. A started cycle
  always finishes, so stock can end up to one cycle's output over the target. At or over the
  target it starts nothing and is idle **"Holding: 50 Iron"**; it stays online and draws its
  capacity, like a processor with an empty queue. An idle fuel burner takes no new fuel, as now.
  Input choices (Auto, or a chosen alternative) apply as in Queue mode.
- **Leaving Hold** (`ClearHold(id)`): back to Queue mode with an empty queue. A running cycle
  finishes and delivers (as one loaded from a save before the queue does), then it is idle "Queue
  empty".
- **The target:**
  - Entering Hold, or picking another recipe in Hold, sets it to what storage holds of the output
    now (50 Iron in stock means hold at 50), and at least one cycle's output (so a Smelter with no
    Iron in stock holds at 1).
  - "-" and "+" step it by one cycle's output (the hold-to-repeat step buttons), from one cycle's
    output up to what storage could hold of it (what it holds plus its room, `RoomFor`; for a
    Cargo resource, the shared pool's room at its weight). The bound is read when stepping, so a
    target above a since-shrunk limit stays until stepped.
- **The detail panel,** for a queueing processor:
  - A **Mode** picker (`buildingDetail/mode`: "Queue", "Hold"), like the Recipe and Fuel pickers,
    right under the Recipe picker. It stands in for a switch until the toggle control is decided
    (see Notes).
  - In Queue mode, everything is as now (quantity, Craft, the queue list).
  - In Hold mode the Recipe picker chooses the held recipe, and in place of the quantity, Craft,
    and queue: a "Hold at" row with "-", the target, and "+" (`buildingDetail/holdLess`,
    `buildingDetail/holdMore`), and under it a dim line, "In storage: 32 / 50 Iron", the 32 in
    `Text` color when at or over the target. The State row shows "Holding: 50 Iron" or the
    cycle's progress, as now.
- **Saving:** a building saves its hold (recipe and target); older saves have none (Queue mode).

## Notes

- The Mode picker is the stopgap the toggle decision may replace. A later toggle would read
  "Queue" / "Hold" and swap in for the picker with no change to the sim.

## Code changes

- `src/Telaelus.Sim/Building.cs`: `Hold` (recipe, target).
- `src/Telaelus.Sim/Simulation.cs` (or `SimulationRecipes.cs`): `SetHold`, `ClearHold`, the hold
  rule in choosing the next cycle, and the "Holding" status.
- `src/Telaelus.Sim/SaveData.cs`, `SimulationSave.cs`: the hold.
- `src/Telaelus/Presentation/Ui/BuildingDetail.cs`: the Mode picker, the Hold at row (two
  `StepButton`s), and the storage line.
- `src/Telaelus/Testing/StateSnapshot.cs`: `buildings[n].hold` (`{ recipe, target }` or null),
  `ui.mode`, and `ui.hold` (`{ target, line }`).
- Sim tests (`CraftingQueueTests` or a new `HoldTests`): entering Hold empties the queue and
  returns a running other-recipe cycle's inputs; a held Smelter makes Iron until storage holds the
  target, then idles "Holding" and still draws; using Iron from storage starts it again; a cycle
  started under the target finishes past it; the target defaults to the stock (and at least one
  cycle); leaving Hold finishes the running cycle and leaves an empty queue; a save keeps the
  hold.
- Scenario: a new `hold-mode` scenario (a Smelter set to Hold at 3 Iron from the panel, stepping
  the target with + and -, idling "Holding: 3 Iron", resuming when Iron is taken from storage, and
  back to Queue), with windowed screenshots of both modes.

## Verification

- Sim and integration tests pass.
- Windowed: a Smelter in Hold keeps Iron at its target as Iron is spent elsewhere.

## Implementation notes

- `HoldOrder` is its own type beside `CraftOrder`; `WantsCycle` is the one check for "start a
  cycle" in both modes, and the Holding status sits beside "Queue empty".
- The target's top is capped at 9999 for a resource storage does not limit.
- Scenarios had no way to choose from a picker, so a `selectUi` `{ id, item }` step was added
  (sets the item and raises `ItemSelected`, as choosing it from the list does).
- The windowed screenshots show the panel's Mode picker and Hold at row; at the default window
  height the storage line sits just below the fold of the scrolling body.
