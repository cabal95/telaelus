# Power

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611142117-power
- Depends-on: none

## Summary

Adds Power as the network's first Capacity resource: online generators supply it, online powered
buildings draw it, and a shortage slows every consumer (a brownout) and then stops them all (a
blackout). Adds the fuel slot and its picker (the Smelter now burns fuel too), two generators
(the Water Wheel and the Burner Generator), and the first powered tier: the Lumber Mill and the
Drill Rig, faster forms of the Lumber Camp and Mine, and the Refinery, which now needs power. Power
shows on the top bar and in the building panel, colored by state.

## Motivation

The third follow-on of 2611141757-resource-tree (section 5, the Fuel and Input pickers parts of
section 1, and decisions 3, 4, 15, and 16 there). Tier-0 buildings are slow by design; power is
the step that makes them faster, and it is the first thing the HQ network carries besides storage
access. It also lands the fuel mechanic that the Smelter and later generators share.

## Design

### Scope

- **In:** the Capacity model for Power on the HQ network; the brownout and blackout rule; fuel
  slots, burn times, and the fuel picker, on the Burner Generator and the Smelter; the Water Wheel,
  Burner Generator, Lumber Mill, and Drill Rig with their models; the Refinery drawing power; power
  in the top bar, building panel, and placement tooltip.
- **Out:** Water and Sand capacity (terrain-capacity); the other generators (Oil Generator, Solar
  Array, Geothermal Tap, Fission Reactor) and the other powered buildings (Electric Furnace, Steel
  Mill, Chemical Plant, Assembler), which arrive with the production tiers (the Refinery is the
  one existing building that joins the powered tier now); Charcoal as a fuel in
  practice (its Kiln comes with renewables, though the picker already lists it).

### Supply and demand

- **Supply** is the sum of every online, switched-on generator's output while it runs (a Burner
  Generator only while it has fuel burning).
- A **Burner Generator burns fuel whenever it is switched on** and has fuel, whether or not anything
  draws power and whether or not it is online; switching it off is how the player saves fuel. Only
  an online one adds to supply.
- **Demand** is the sum of every online, switched-on powered building's draw, whether or not it is
  working at the moment, so a blackout cannot flap on and off.
- **Overload** is `demand / supply - 1`. Every powered consumer runs at `1 - 2 x overload` of its
  speed: full speed at or under supply, 80% at 10% over, 50% at 25% over. Past 25% over (or with
  demand and no supply) it is a **blackout**: every powered consumer stops until the player adds
  supply or switches buildings off.
- **Offline** powered buildings get no power: they are idle with "No power: offline". Offline
  generators supply nothing.
- Unpowered buildings (tier 0, storage, hubs, generators) are unaffected by any of this.
- The network keeps `PowerSupply`, `PowerDemand`, and `PowerSpeed` (the consumer multiplier),
  recomputed each tick from the online buildings (one pass, the same loop that steps them).

| State | Demand | Example | Readout color | Consumer speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | up to supply | 45 / 100 | normal text | 100% |
| Brownout | up to 25% over | 110 / 100 | `Warning` | 100% minus twice the overload |
| Blackout | more than 25% over | 140 / 100 | `Danger` | 0 |

### Fuel

- A **fuel-burning building** has a fuel slot. It takes one unit of fuel, burns it for that fuel's
  **burn time** while it runs, then takes the next. A unit being burned is spent: switching off or
  destroying the building does not return it.
- Burn times (placeholders): Wood 10 s, Charcoal 30 s, Coal 40 s. `ResourceDef` gains a burn time;
  a resource with one is a fuel.
- **Fuel picker** in the building panel: **Best** (the default: the longest-burning fuel storage
  holds, falling back when it runs out) or one fuel by name. Its tooltip names the current choice
  ("Best: burns Coal (40 s per unit)").
- A fuel burner takes its next unit from its fuel slot first and then, online, from storage.
  Offline, the player loads its fuel slot by hand with E, like an offline processor's inputs (a
  fuel slot of 5 units); a Smelter loads its recipe inputs before its fuel. Unburned slot units are
  refunded on destroy; the unit burning is not.
- **The Smelter burns fuel** (Coal, Charcoal, or Wood) on top of its ore: a cycle advances only while
  a unit is burning, and it takes the next unit when one burns out mid-cycle, so a 16 s cycle uses
  0.4 Coal or 1.6 Wood. With no fuel it is idle "Waiting for fuel" (offline: "Waiting for fuel (load
  by hand)"). It stays unpowered (tier 0). A unit burning when a cycle ends keeps burning only while
  the next cycle runs; between cycles it waits, half-burned, in the slot.

### The powered tier (placeholders)

| Building | Footprint | Cost | Draw | Speed | Works | Buffer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lumber Mill | 3 x 3 | 30 Wood, 20 Iron | 5 | 2.0 | Wood, radius 8 | 40 |
| Drill Rig | 3 x 3 | 30 Iron, 20 Stone | 10 | 2.0 | Iron Ore, Copper Ore, Coal, Stone, radius 8 | 40 |

- **The Refinery** draws 25 and is otherwise unchanged, so the oil chain now needs a generator
  first.
- At speed 2.0 a Lumber Mill gathers Wood every 1.5 s and a Drill Rig Iron Ore every 3 s, against
  4 s and 8 s for the Lumber Camp and Mine. Both are extractors, so they fill their buffer like any
  collector (hand collection, then trucks).
- `BuildingDef` gains `Tier`, `PowerDraw`, `PowerSupply`, and `Fuel` (whether it has a fuel slot).
  A building is powered when its draw is above 0; the exemptions (storage, hubs, generators) are
  just definitions with no draw.

### UI

- **Top bar:** "Power 45 / 100" on the right, before the launch progress, in the state's color; in
  a blackout it reads "Blackout: Power 140 / 100".
- **Building panel:** the panel's old Power row (switched on or off) is renamed **Switch**, so
  **Power** can mean electricity: a powered building shows "Draws 10", "Draws 10 (running at
  80%)", "Draws 10: blackout", or "No power: offline"; a generator "Supplies 100" or "Offline:
  supplies nothing". A building with a fuel slot (the Burner Generator, the Smelter) has a
  "Burning" row ("Coal, 22 s left; 4 in slot") and a Fuel picker with a dim note naming what it
  burns ("Best: burns Coal (40 s per unit)") instead of a hover tooltip, so it reads without
  hovering. The progress bar shows only for extractors and processors, not generators.
- **Placement tooltip:** for a powered building or generator, its draw or supply and the network's
  power after placing it ("Power 55 / 100").
- **Network overlay:** unchanged; generators are ordinary covered buildings.

### Assets

Four building models, each with its accent color, kept preview sheets, and README rows:

- **Water Wheel:** a wooden paddle wheel on an axle between two stone piers, with a small
  generator housing on one bank (`water_wheel_accent`, blue).
- **Burner Generator:** a squat boiler with a chimney, a fuel hopper at the front, and a coil of
  cables to a yellow junction box (`burner_accent`, orange-red).
- **Lumber Mill:** a covered saw hall with a conveyor of logs out the front and a power pole
  (`lumber_accent` brown, with a yellow powered stripe, `power_accent`).
- **Drill Rig:** a steel derrick over a drill head, a motor house, and a power pole (`mine_accent`
  orange, with `power_accent`).

`power_accent` (electric yellow) marks every powered building, so the powered tier reads as one
family.

## Verification

- Unit tests: supply and demand sums over online buildings only; speed at 0%, 10%, 25%, and over
  25% overload; demand counting switched-on consumers that are idle; a blackout holding until
  supply rises or demand falls; offline consumers and generators; the Burner Generator burning
  whenever on, by burn time, and the Best picker falling back; a burning unit not returned; the
  Smelter's cycle advancing only while fuel burns and stopping without it; the Refinery stopping
  without power;
  the Water Wheel's placement on Straight river cells only; the Lumber Mill and Drill Rig gathering
  at their speed scaled by power.
- Scenario: a `power` scenario places a Drill Rig with no power ("Blackout" with no supply), adds
  a Burner Generator with Coal and sees it run, overloads it into brownout and blackout and checks
  the top bar colors, and opens the generator's panel to switch its fuel.
- Both test suites pass; a windowed run shows the four models and the power readout.

## Decisions

1. **The Smelter burns fuel** from this changeset on.
2. **The Refinery draws power** (25) from this changeset on.
3. **The Burner Generator burns fuel whenever it is switched on**, with or without demand.
4. **The Water Wheel is 4 x 2** across a Straight river map cell.
5. **Generator costs are doubled** from the first draft: Water Wheel 40 Wood, 20 Stone; Burner
   Generator 60 Stone, 40 Iron. Both still fit the HQ's base storage.
