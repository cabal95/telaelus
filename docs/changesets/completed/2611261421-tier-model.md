# Tier Model

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611261421-tier-model

## Summary

Buildings, items, and research share one set of four tiers. Tier 1 buildings can be built from
the start; tiers 2, 3, and 4 unlock through one research project each (Steel Works, Electronics,
Computers). A tier N building makes tier N items. The "hold a building's whole cost once" unlock
and its "New building" notification go. In their place, finishing a tier's project shows "Tier 2
buildings and research are now available". This is phase 1 of the tier rework plan
(future/tier-rework.md). Phase 2 adds the tier pickers and the grayed and hidden rows.

## Motivation

Building tiers exist today but do nothing in play, and resource tiers don't line up with them.
Tier 1 items come from both unpowered and powered buildings. Unlocking a building by holding its
cost gives the player a stream of one-off notifications and no sense of stages. With one tier
model, "Tier 2" means the same stage of the game everywhere, and research becomes how the player
moves between stages.

## Design

### Resource tiers

Raw resources stay tier 0, including Lithium, which is terrain-sourced. Today's tiers 1 to 4 are
re-cut so that resource tier N is what tier N buildings make:

| Tier | Items |
|---|---|
| 1 | Iron, Copper, Glass, Charcoal, Lumber, Cement |
| 2 | Steel, Concrete, Kerosene, Plastic, Rubber, Carbon Fiber, Sulfuric Acid, Hydrogen, Liquid Oxygen, Rocket Fuel, Aluminum, Silicon, Titanium |
| 3 | Hull Plate, Steel Beam, Copper Wire, Circuit, Insulated Cable, Machine Parts, Silicon Wafer, Processor, Magnet, Electric Motor, Battery, Solar Cell, Composite Panel, Ceramic Tile, Aluminum Frame, Armor Plate |
| 4 | Control Module, Computer, Thruster Assembly, Fuel Tank, Heat Shield, Fuel Rod, Life Support Unit, Sensor Array, Hull Section, Engine, Enriched Uranium |

`ResourceDefs.MaxTier` stays 4. The inventory's tier headings follow the new tiers with no other
change.

### Building tiers

`BuildingDef.Tier` becomes 1 to 4 for every buildable type. The HQ is tier 1, and roads and
bridges are always available. **Gateways** are built from the tier below. The internal names
(Settlement, Industry, Components, Assembly) are for docs and code only; the player sees only
"Tier 1" to "Tier 4".

| Tier | Buildings |
|---|---|
| 1 (Settlement) | Mine, Lumber Camp, Smelter, Glassworks, Charcoal Kiln, Sawmill, Cement Kiln, Water Wheel, Burner Generator, Water Pump, Sand Collector, Depot, Silo, Stockyard, Warehouse, Small Hub, Relay, Research Lab |
| 2 (Industry) | Gateways: Steel Mill, Refinery, Oil Well, Drill Rig. Then Tank Farm, Vault, Lumber Mill, Pumping Station, Sand Dredge, Rock Crusher, Quarry, Forester, Deep Well, Concrete Mixer, Chemical Plant, Electrolyzer, Fuel Plant, Aluminum Smelter, Electric Furnace, Brine Well |
| 3 (Components) | Gateways: Fabricator, Electronics Works. Then Tile Kiln, Semiconductor Fab, Air Separator, Deep Mine, Solar Array, Battery Bank |
| 4 (Assembly) | Gateway: Assembly Plant. Then Enrichment Plant, Fission Reactor, Shipyard |

### Building costs

Most costs stay as they are. Five change:

| Building | Today | New |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Well | 60 Iron, 30 Stone | 40 Steel, 30 Stone |
| Deep Mine | 80 Iron, 40 Stone | 60 Steel, 30 Concrete |
| Rock Crusher | 40 Iron, 20 Stone | 30 Iron, 10 Steel, 20 Stone |
| Pumping Station | 40 Iron, 20 Stone | 30 Iron, 10 Steel, 20 Stone |
| Sand Dredge | 40 Iron, 20 Stone | 30 Iron, 10 Steel, 20 Stone |

### Unlocking

- **The unlocked tier** (`Simulation.UnlockedTier`, 1 to 4) is 1 plus the number of tier unlock
  projects completed. It is derived from research, never stored.
- **A building is unlocked** (`IsUnlocked`) when it is buildable and its tier is at or below the
  unlocked tier. Placing still requires it to be unlocked and affordable. Buildings already placed
  in a tier above the unlocked tier keep working. They can be destroyed but not placed again.
- **Removed:** the unlocked-building set, `UnlockAffordable`, and `SimEventKind.Unlocked`.

### Research

- **Every project gets a tier** (`ResearchDef.Tier`). A project is Locked while its tier is above
  the unlocked tier or while a prerequisite is incomplete. Material Transfer is tier 1 and Advanced
  Material Transfer is tier 2.
- **Unlock projects:** a project may unlock a tier (`ResearchDef.Unlocks`). There are three new
  ones. Each fee is paid in items from the project's own tier, plus Wood. Times are work at one
  lab's speed. The fees are modest for now, because these projects will later gain prerequisites.

  | Project | Tier | Needs | Unlocks | Fee | Work | Tree place |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Steel Works | 1 | | Tier 2 | 60 Iron, 60 Copper, 40 Glass, 80 Wood | 20 min | column 0, row 1 |
  | Electronics | 2 | Steel Works | Tier 3 | 60 Steel, 60 Plastic, 40 Aluminum, 120 Wood | 30 min | column 1, row 1 |
  | Computers | 3 | Electronics | Tier 4 | 60 Circuit, 20 Electric Motor, 40 Steel, 200 Wood | 45 min | column 2, row 1 |

- **Descriptions:** "Unlocks Tier 2 buildings: steel, oil, and the chemistry they open up.",
  "Unlocks Tier 3 buildings: components, circuits, and motors.", and "Unlocks Tier 4 buildings:
  assemblies, reactors, and the Shipyard."
- **Icons:** each new project gets an icon model (`icons/steel_works`, `icons/electronics`,
  `icons/computers`). They come from the icon generator, with kept previews.

### Notifications

- Completing an unlock project shows its "Research complete" toast as any project does, and a
  second lasting toast, "Tier 2 buildings and research are now available", with the detail "[B]
  to build".
- The "New building" toasts are gone. Discovery toasts are unchanged.

### Build menu

Phase 1 shows a locked tier's buildings in the menu as today's undiscovered cells: dark, nameless,
and sorted last. Phase 2 replaces this with the tier picker.

### Trainer

The Lumber Camp step no longer waits for an unlock. "Walk to the trees and hold [E] to gather
Wood." now completes when storage can afford the Lumber Camp. Its hint becomes "Gather 15 to
build the Lumber Camp."

### Saves and the demo town

- **Saves:** the unlocked-building list is no longer written. An old save's list is ignored on
  load, and its tier comes from its research only. Its placed buildings stay, including any above
  that tier. The format version is unchanged, because an old save still loads.
- **Demo town:** it has a Drill Rig (tier 2), so it completes Steel Works (paying the fee and
  doing the work) before placing its buildings. It then starts Material Transfer as it does now.
  The demo save is rewritten.

## Verification

- Sim and integration tests pass.
- Windowed: a new game builds a Smelter, a Glassworks and a Research Lab, but no Steel Mill. Seeding
  the fee and finishing Steel Works shows the tier toast, and the Steel Mill can then be placed.
- The demo save loads with its Drill Rig working and tier 2 unlocked.

## Notes

- Phase 2 (visibility and pickers) is its own changeset: raw resources grayed until found, items
  hidden until their tier and then grayed until made, the build menu and research tier pickers
  (with the green check mark for a completed tier), and the inventory stepper.

## Implementation notes

- The `ResourceType` enum is reordered to the new tiers (a test keeps it in tier order). Saves
  store resources by name, so old saves are unaffected; `previews/items.png` is rerendered in the
  new order, and the top bar's pins and inventory rows follow it.
- `UnlockedTier` lives with the research state (`SimulationResearch.cs`). `BuildingDef.Tier`
  defaults to 1, so only tiers 2 to 4 are written out.
- The extractor speed test now reads "a powered extractor is fast" (2) rather than "tier 1 and
  up", since the Oil Well is tier 2 but unpowered at the base speed.
- Saves need no format change: `System.Text.Json` ignores the old `Unlocked` list.
- Both toasts use the project's icon. The tier toast comes after the research toast, so it shows
  on top of the stack.
- The trainer's gather step now finishes when the Lumber Camp is affordable.
- The build menu's tier 1 cells now all show from the start, so the `build-menu` scenario's expected
  cells changed with it.
