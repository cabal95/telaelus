# Renewables

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611150937-renewables
- Depends-on: none

## Summary

Adds the renewable bypasses for finite resources, so a near map that runs dry stays worth building
on: the **Forester** is a tree plot that regrows its own Wood field, the **Quarry** cuts endless Stone (or Limestone in the
Highlands) from a mountain face, the **Charcoal Kiln** turns Wood into Charcoal (the renewable
fuel), the **Rock Crusher** turns Stone into Sand capacity anywhere, and the **Deep Well** and
**Deep Mine** keep worked-out fields producing slowly. A depleted field now leaves a
**worked-out** marker on its map cell for them. Rare **motherlodes**, endless sites that can
yield any of several resources, appear across the map; one Deep Mine or Deep Well works each, one
chosen resource at a time, so a growing base needs several.

## Motivation

The eighth follow-on of 2611141757-resource-tree (section 9 there). The field rebalance made fields
fewer and larger, so a base eventually exhausts what is near it; section 9's rule for a bypass is
that it reads as physically plausible, costs more than mining a field (a higher tier, power, or a
site requirement), and arrives mid or late game. The Rock Crusher was deferred here by
2611142141-terrain-capacity (decision 3 there) as the Sand bypass for a map without Sand terrain.
Motherlodes give the late game a reason to extend the network across the map.

## Design

### Scope

- **In:** the six buildings below with their models; worked-out markers on depleted fields, with
  their models; the Forester's own Wood field, growing and regrowing; collectors that produce
  without a field, served by trucks and hand collection like extractors; motherlodes, their generation, and
  their model.
- **Out:** Charcoal as a Coal substitute in recipes (Steel and Silicon come with the production
  tiers; Charcoal already burns as fuel); anything that uses Limestone; draining collectors into
  storage over the network (Material Transfer, later research).

### The buildings (placeholders)

| Building | Footprint | Cost | Tier, Power | Placement | Does |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forester | 4 x 4 | 30 Wood, 20 Stone | 1, draws 2 Power and 5 Water | Exactly over an open Grass map cell | Grows a 150-Wood field on its own cell; regrows it 30 s after it is cut down |
| Quarry | 3 x 3 | 40 Iron, 30 Wood | 1, draws 10 | At least one cell edge-adjacent to a mountain | 1 Stone (or Limestone) every 6 s, forever; buffer 40 |
| Charcoal Kiln | 2 x 2 | 30 Stone, 10 Wood | 0 | Standard | 3 Wood to 1 Charcoal every 15 s; buffer 10, inputs 5 cycles |
| Rock Crusher | 3 x 3 | 40 Iron, 20 Stone | 1, draws 15 | Standard | Supplies 4 Sand, eating 1 Stone every 10 s |
| Deep Well | 4 x 4 | 60 Iron, 30 Stone | 1, draws 15 | Exactly over a worked-out Crude Oil cell or a Salt Dome | Worked-out: 1 Crude Oil every 26.7 s; Salt Dome: its chosen yield (below); forever; buffer 20 |
| Deep Mine | 4 x 4 | 80 Iron, 40 Stone | 2, draws 30 | Exactly over a worked-out ore cell (below) or a solid motherlode | Worked-out: 1 unit every 20 s; motherlode: its chosen yield (below); forever; buffer 40 |

**Forester.**
- **A tree plot you build:** 4 x 4, covering one map cell exactly, like the Deep Well and Deep
  Mine; its ghost snaps to map cells and is green over an **open Grass cell** (every fine cell
  Grass terrain, buildable, free, explored, no field, no feature).
- **Its field:** the Forester owns a Wood field on its own map cell. Placed, it plants at once:
  the field is **growing** for 180 s, then grown, holding 150 Wood. A grown field is an ordinary
  Wood field: Lumber Camps and Lumber Mills in range work it, and the player harvests it by hand
  from beside the plot. A growing field cannot be worked or harvested.
- **Regrowing:** when the field is cut down to nothing, the plot stands bare for 30 s, then plants
  again and grows for another 180 s. Cutting a field down leaves no worked-out marker (it is Wood).
- **What it needs:** growth and the 30 s wait advance only while it is switched on and online, at
  its work speed: the slower of its Power (2) and Water (5) speeds, so a Water brownout slows the
  trees and a blackout stops them. 5 Water is more than a Water Pump supplies (4), so each
  Forester takes two Pumps, or a Pumping Station (16) runs three.
- **Pace:** a cycle is 30 s bare, 180 s growing, and the cutting: a Lumber Camp fells 150 Wood in
  about 13 minutes and a Lumber Mill in about 5, so a plot gives roughly 150 Wood every 8 to 17
  minutes, and a base that burns or builds with Wood runs several.
- **Destroyed,** it takes its field with it (Wood still on it is lost, like a field).

**Quarry.**
- A collector with no field: it produces at its own rate into its buffer, and stops when the
  buffer is full ("Full: waiting for pickup"), like an extractor. It works the mountain it stands
  beside, which never runs out.
- **Its output** is Stone, or Limestone when it stands beside a mountain in the Highlands: the
  panel has an Output picker (Stone, Limestone) there, defaulting to Stone, and shows only Stone
  elsewhere. One output at a time, per the plan's one-output rule.

**Charcoal Kiln.**
- A processor like the Smelter but with no fuel slot (part of the wood burns to char the rest, so
  3 Wood make 1 Charcoal). 3 Wood burn 30 s and 1 Charcoal burns 30 s, so it gains no fuel time;
  what it gains is density (a third the storage) and, with the production tiers, the Coal
  substitute for Steel and Silicon.

**Rock Crusher.**
- A Sand provider that runs on Stone: while it runs it takes 1 Stone from storage every 10 s and
  supplies 4 Sand, scaled by its power speed like the Sand Dredge. With no Stone in storage it
  supplies nothing ("Waiting for Stone"). Offline it supplies nothing, like any provider.
- So a map with no Sand terrain can still run a Glassworks, at the cost of power and Stone.

**Deep Well and Deep Mine.**
- **Each covers its site exactly:** 4 x 4, placed only on the 16 fine cells of a worked-out map
  cell it can work, or on the **center 4 x 4 of a motherlode** it can work (motherlodes are 8 x 8,
  below). Being square, any facing fits.
- **The ghost snaps:** while either is selected, its ghost snaps to the center of a motherlode
  when the pointer is over one, and to the map cell under the pointer otherwise (green over a site
  it can work, red elsewhere), so the player never lines it up by hand.
- **Reaching it on a motherlode:** the motherlode's ring around the building stays solid ground
  nobody walks on, so trucks drive to a cell beside the motherlode and the player collects (E) from
  beside the motherlode; for a building over a motherlode, "beside" means beside the motherlode.
- **The Deep Well** works a worked-out Crude Oil cell (at 15% of an Oil Well's rate: an Oil Well
  takes 4 s a unit, the Deep Well 26.7 s) or a Salt Dome motherlode.
- **The Deep Mine** works a worked-out Iron Ore, Copper Ore, Bauxite, Titanium Ore, Quartz, or
  Limestone cell (1 unit of that deposit every 20 s, about half a base Mine) or any solid
  motherlode. Coal has Charcoal, Stone the Quarry, Sulfur its motherlodes, and
  Rare Earths, Uranium Ore, and Crystals no bypass on the planet (they pull toward Part B), so a
  worked-out cell of those takes no Deep Mine.
- Both are collectors with no field, at tier 1 (the Deep Well) and tier 2 (the Deep Mine, the
  first tier-2 building), on power.

### Motherlodes

- **A motherlode** is a rare kind of field: **8 x 8** (2 x 2 map cells, aligned to the map grid),
  twice the side of an ordinary field so it stands out on the map. It holds no amount and never
  depletes, nothing but a Deep Mine or Deep Well on its center works it (not the player by hand,
  not a Mine or Drill Rig), and it can yield **any of several resources**. Its biome decides its
  kind:

| Motherlode | Biomes | Yields (share) | Worked by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laterite | Grassland, Desert | Iron Ore 60%, Bauxite 40% | Deep Mine |
| Porphyry | Highlands | Copper Ore 55%, Iron Ore 25%, Quartz 20% | Deep Mine |
| Coal Measures | Highlands, Forest | Coal 50%, Iron Ore 30%, Limestone 20% | Deep Mine |
| Salt Dome | Desert | Crude Oil 75%, Sulfur 25% | Deep Well |
| Mineral Sands | Tundra | Titanium Ore 50%, Quartz 40%, Rare Earths 10% | Deep Mine |
| Massive Sulfide | Volcanic, Swamp | Sulfur 45%, Copper Ore 35%, Iron Ore 20% | Deep Mine |

- The plan put the Salt Dome and Mineral Sands on the Ocean shore, but Ocean cells are water and
  take no fields, so the Salt Dome stays in the Desert and Mineral Sands go to the Tundra (where
  Titanium Ore, Quartz, and Rare Earths fields already are).
- **One building, one resource at a time.** A motherlode is worked by at most one building: the
  Deep Mine or Deep Well on its center 4 x 4, so no second one fits. That building yields
  **one chosen resource** of the motherlode's, never a mix, so one motherlode never supplies a
  whole chain, and a base that needs Iron Ore and Bauxite at once needs two Laterites (or a field).
  Finding one is a boost, not a base for life.
- **The choice is the building's:** the Deep Mine's or Deep Well's panel has an Output picker
  listing the motherlode's yields (the largest share first, and the default), like the Quarry's.
  Changing it takes effect on the next unit; what is in the buffer stays.
- **The shares set the rates:** the largest share yields 1 unit every 5 s; a smaller one is slower
  in proportion (5 s times the largest share over its share). Laterite gives Iron Ore every 5 s or
  Bauxite every 7.5 s; Mineral Sands gives Titanium Ore every 5 s, Quartz every 6.25 s, or Rare
  Earths every 25 s; the Salt Dome Crude Oil every 5 s or Sulfur every 15 s. So the mix still says
  what a motherlode is rich in, and its rare yields stay rare.
- **Generation:** each chunk has a 1 in 5 chance (by a hash of the seed) of one motherlode, on a
  block of 2 x 2 map cells (with the ring of cells around it) inside the chunk, all four open
  (walkable, no feature; a building on a motherlode stands on the motherlode, so its ground need
  not be buildable, which lets Swamp mud take a Massive Sulfide) and of biomes
  that host the same kind, with no field on any of the 12 cells around the block, at least 24 map
  cells from the spawn. The block's kind comes from its biome; where the biome hosts two kinds, a
  second hash picks. Rolled fields that would touch it are dropped. The test map gets one Laterite
  (on map cells (11..12, 13..14); its Limestone moves to (6, 12) and its Titanium Ore to (13, 11)
  to keep clear)
  motherlode for tests, on Grassland.
- **Multi-cell fields:** a motherlode is the first field covering more than one map cell. The
  field index lists it under all four cells, so `FieldAt`, placement, walking, and its views treat
  every one of its 64 fine cells as the field.
- **Discovery:** seeing a motherlode discovers each resource it can yield.

### Worked-out markers

- When a field of a deposit other than Wood depletes, its map cell keeps a **worked-out** record of
  the deposit type (`WorkedOut`), and the world shows a marker there: a dry dark stain for Crude
  Oil, a shallow pit ringed with spoil for the solid deposits.
- A marker blocks nothing: the cell is walkable and buildable as before. Building over it leaves
  the record (the Deep Well and Deep Mine need it underneath). Hovering it names it ("Worked-out
  Crude Oil").
- Wood leaves no marker: a felled field simply clears (a Forester can replant there).

### Collectors without a field

- A **collector** is now any building that produces into its output buffer without a recipe: the
  extractors and the Quarry, Deep Well, and Deep Mine. Trucks dispatch to every collector, hand
  collection (E) works on every collector, and their buffers drain the same way.

### UI

- **Building panel:** the Forester shows its plot ("Growing: 2:10 left", "Grown: 112 / 150 Wood",
  or "Replanting in 18 s") and its Power and Water rows; the Quarry its Output picker where Limestone is offered; the Rock
  Crusher an Eats row ("1 Stone every 10 s; 230 in storage"); a Deep Well or Deep Mine a Site
  row ("Worked-out Iron Ore", "Laterite motherlode") and, on a motherlode, its Output picker with
  each choice's rate ("Iron Ore, every 5 s").
- **Field panel:** a Forester's growing field shows "Growing: 2:10 left" instead of Remaining and
  Worked by (clicking the plot opens the Forester's panel, which shows the same); a motherlode its kind as the title, its yields with their rates, "Endless", and Worked by (its
  one building, and what that is set to).
- **Placement:** the Quarry's ghost is red away from a mountain; the Forester's, Deep Well's, and
  Deep Mine's snap to map cells (and the deep buildings' to a motherlode's center) and are green
  only exactly over a site they can use.

### Assets

Six building models and three site models, each building with an accent color, kept preview
sheets, and README rows:

- **Forester (4 x 4):** a low fence around a tilled plot, a small potting shed with a green roof
  and a water tank at one corner, and a power pole (`forester_accent`, a leaf green). The trees are
  not in the model: the plot draws its field's tree models (broadleaf) at the growth stage, so it
  stands bare, then saplings, then full trees, then thins as it is cut.
- **Quarry (3 x 3):** a cut stone face on a squared block at the back (the side that meets the
  mountain), a small derrick crane lifting a block, stacked cut blocks at the front, and a power
  pole (`quarry_accent`, a slate blue-grey).
- **Charcoal Kiln (2 x 2):** two domed brick beehive kilns with dark doors and smoke vents, a
  stack of logs, and a heap of charcoal (`kiln_accent`, a brick red-brown).
- **Rock Crusher (3 x 3):** a hopper over a boxy jaw crusher, a short conveyor out to a sand pile,
  and a power pole (`sand_accent` with `power_accent`).
- **Deep Well (4 x 4):** a tall derrick at the center over a capped wellhead on an oil-stained
  pad (the old seep), a pump motor and a short tank to one side, a pipe rack, and a power pole
  (`oil_well_accent`, shared with the Oil Well).
- **Deep Mine (4 x 4):** a steel headframe tower with a winding wheel over a shaft at the center, a
  winding house, an ore skip on rails to a tipple at the front, and a power pole (`mine_accent`
  with `power_accent`).
- **Site models:** `worked_out_oil` (a flat dry stain, about 0.02 high, filling most of the cell),
  `worked_out_pit` (a shallow pit with a low spoil rim, about 0.2 high), and `motherlode_base` (a
  broad dark rock mound, about 0.5 high, filling 8 x 8 with a flatter center where its building
  stands). A motherlode draws the base with its yields' field models (and the Salt Dome a Crude Oil
  pool) set around its ring, so it reads as a rich outcrop at a glance, and its building sits in
  the middle.
- **Growing trees** reuse the existing tree models, scaled by growth (a third of full height at
  planting, rising to full), inside the Forester's fence.

## Verification

- Unit tests: a depleted Iron Ore field leaving a worked-out record and a depleted Wood field none;
  a Forester only exactly over an open Grass cell, its field growing 180 s and unworkable until
  grown, holding 150, felled by a Lumber Camp beside it, regrowing 30 s after, growing slower in a
  Water brownout and not at all in a blackout or offline, and gone with the building; a Quarry only beside a mountain, producing Stone forever and Limestone by
  choice only in the Highlands; the Charcoal Kiln's recipe; a Deep Well only exactly over a
  worked-out Crude Oil cell or a Salt Dome (refused one fine cell off, and over a worked-out Iron
  Ore cell) and at its rates; a Deep Mine over worked-out Iron Ore and over a Laterite motherlode,
  refused over worked-out Coal; a motherlode yielding only the chosen resource at its share's
  rate, switching on the next unit, and never depleting; a Deep Mine accepted only on a
  motherlode's center 4 x 4 (refused one fine cell off); `FieldAt` finding the motherlode from all
  64 of its cells; a truck and the player collecting from a Deep Mine on a motherlode; a Mine, a Drill Rig, and the player unable to work a motherlode; motherlodes
  generating on several seeds, in their biomes, apart from the spawn; the Rock Crusher supplying
  Sand while it has Stone and power, eating Stone, and supplying nothing without; trucks and E
  serving the Quarry.
- Scenario: a `renewables` scenario places a Forester with Water Pumps and a Lumber Camp beside it
  and sees its trees grow, be felled, and regrow; a Quarry beside a mountain filling its buffer; a Glassworks running on a Rock Crusher;
  a Deep Mine on the test map's Laterite motherlode.
- Both test suites pass; a windowed run shows the six models, a marker, a motherlode, and
  growing trees.

## Decisions

1. **The Deep Well and Deep Mine are 4 x 4 and cover their site exactly:** a worked-out map cell, or a motherlode's center 4 x 4 (decision 12).
2. **The Forester, Quarry, Rock Crusher, and Deep Well are tier 1 on power** (the Deep Mine tier 2).
3. **The Quarry's Limestone is an Output picker** beside a Highlands mountain.
4. **The Forester is a 4 x 4 tree plot** over an open Grass cell, drawing 2 Power and 5 Water: its
   own field grows 150 Wood in 180 s, is cut by Lumber Camps, Lumber Mills, or the player, and
   regrows 30 s after it is cut down. (It replaced a Forester that planted saplings around it.)
5. **The Deep Mine and motherlodes come in this changeset.**
6. **Motherlodes are endless, worked by at most one building, which yields one chosen resource at a
   time,** so a base keeps looking for more.
7. **Motherlodes are rare:** a 1 in 5 chance per chunk, at least 24 map cells from the spawn.
8. **The deep rates:** 1 unit every 20 s on a worked-out ore cell, 26.7 s on a worked-out seep, and 5
   s on a motherlode (its largest share).
9. **The Salt Dome is in the Desert only and Mineral Sands in the Tundra,** since Ocean cells take no
   fields.
10. **The resource choice is an Output picker on the Deep Mine or Deep Well** working the motherlode;
    no new building and no setting on the motherlode.
11. **The shares set the rates:** the largest share every 5 s, smaller shares slower in proportion.
12. **Motherlodes are 8 x 8** (2 x 2 map cells) so they are easy to spot, with **one Deep Mine or
    Deep Well on the center 4 x 4.**
