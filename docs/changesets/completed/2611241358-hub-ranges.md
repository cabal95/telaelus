# Longer Hub Ranges and a Cheaper Relay

- Status: Implemented
- Id: 2611241358-hub-ranges

## Summary

Every hub reaches farther. The HQ's coverage and link radii roughly double, the Small Hub's
coverage grows by a quarter and its link range grows so that two linked Small Hubs' coverage just
meets, and the Relay's link range triples. The Relay costs 5 Wood, 10 Stone, and 5 Iron.

## Motivation

Play showed the network takes too many hubs to reach anywhere useful. The HQ covers only a small
patch around itself, a pair of Small Hubs at full link distance leaves an uncovered gap between
them, and a Relay costs 20 Iron to jump only 30 units.

## Design

| Hub | Cost | Coverage radius | Link radius |
|---|---|---|---|
| HQ | placed by the world | 19 → **38** | 22 → **44** |
| Small Hub | 10 Wood, 5 Stone (unchanged) | 9 → **11** | 14 → **22** |
| Relay | 20 Iron, 10 Stone → **5 Wood, 10 Stone, 5 Iron** | 3 (unchanged) | 30 → **90** |

- **HQ:** both radii doubled.
- **Small Hub coverage:** 9 × 1.25 = 11.25, rounded to 11.
- **Small Hub link:** two Small Hubs link when their centers are at most 22 apart. At exactly 22,
  their 11-unit coverage circles meet at the midpoint, so a chain of Small Hubs at full link range
  covers the line between them with no gap. That is a 57% increase, not the ~30% in the request:
  14 × 1.3 ≈ 18 is where the *current* 9-unit circles meet (2 × 9), so the touching rule and the
  larger coverage together give 22.
- **Relay:** link 90, coverage stays 3. The link rule is unchanged (two hubs link when the distance
  between centers is at most the larger of their link radii), so a Relay links to any hub within 90.
- Saves need nothing: radii come from the definitions, and `RecomputeNetwork` runs on load, so a
  loaded game picks up the new reach (buildings that were offline may come online).

- Sim and integration tests pass.
- In game: two Small Hubs placed at the edge of link range show coverage discs that touch.

## Implementation notes

- The HQ's 38 coverage reaches most of the 64-unit test map, so tests that needed a spot out of
  reach moved to its far south-west: the `NetworkTests` link, coverage, branch, and
  `WouldBeOnline` cases, the Storage limits test (its row moved to z 44), and the offline Depot
  test (which now uses a map without the truck tests' hubs).
- The `network` scenario's four-hub chain is one Small Hub now, which shows the longer reach.
- `DemoTown.cs` needed nothing: its buildings were already online, and more reach only adds.
