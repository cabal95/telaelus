# Building models

One model per building type, loaded by `BuildingModels` (`src/Game/Presentation/`) for placed
buildings and the build ghost. Generated by the asset factory
(`src/asset-factory/tools/building_gen.py`, see `docs/asset-pipeline.md`); the look follows
`docs/blueprints/art-style.md`. Every file is required: the game throws if one is missing.

- Authored facing North: the front (the side that marks the facing) is toward -Z.
- The model fills its footprint (1 unit per fine cell) minus a 0.1 inset per side, origin at the
  footprint center on the ground, and is placed unscaled. The game turns it to the building's
  facing.
- Each carries its type's accent color from the palette (Mine orange, Lumber Camp brown, Smelter
  red, Refinery blue, Oil Well yellow, HQ teal, Silo green, Stockyard purple, Tank Farm cyan,
  Warehouse pink, Vault gold); the Small Hub and Relay share the glowing hub green; the Water Wheel is blue and the Burner Generator orange-red, the Water Pump and Pumping Station are light blue (`water_accent`), the Sand Collector and Sand Dredge tan (`sand_accent`), the Glassworks pale cyan (`glass_accent`), and the Depot bright red (`depot_accent`, shared with its trucks); every powered building (the Lumber Mill, Drill Rig, Pumping Station, Sand Dredge, and both generators) carries a yellow `power_accent` mark.
