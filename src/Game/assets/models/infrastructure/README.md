# Infrastructure models

Road and bridge pieces, one per 2 x 2 tile (2 x 2 units), origin at the tile's center on the ground.
Generated by `src/asset-factory/tools/building_gen.py`; kept previews in
`src/asset-factory/previews/` (each sheet and `infrastructure.png`). The game (`RoadViews`) picks a
piece per tile by which of its four edges join another road or bridge tile, and turns it to fit.

Every file is required: the game throws if one is missing.

| Piece | File | Shape | Open edges as authored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patch | `road_patch.glb` | Gravel, 0.03 high, a darker verge all round | none |
| End | `road_end.glb` | Gravel with verges on three sides | North |
| Straight | `road_straight.glb` | Gravel with verges on the East and West | North, South |
| Corner | `road_corner.glb` | Gravel with verges on the South and West | North, East |
| Tee | `road_tee.glb` | Gravel with a verge on the West | North, East, South |
| Crossing | `road_cross.glb` | Gravel, no verges | every edge |
| Bridge deck | `bridge_deck.glb` | Planks across a deck 0.14 up, a rail on posts along each side | runs North-South |
| Bridge ramp | `bridge_ramp.glb` | A deck sloping down to the North onto the bank | runs North-South, low end North |
| Bridge pier | `bridge_pier.glb` | Two piles into the water with a cross beam under the deck | runs North-South (beam across X) |

- North is -Z, East +X. A piece is turned in quarter turns about Y.
- Pebbles on the road pieces sit at the same spots on every piece so neighbors look continuous.
